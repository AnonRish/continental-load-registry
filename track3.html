Evidence semantics
UNKNOWN, PENDING_RESEARCH, SOURCE_AVAILABLE_NOT_INGESTED and NOT_INGESTED are explicit states. They never become evidence of absence. Queue MW, utility service capacity, contracted demand, energized capacity, Epoch IT power, chip power and measured load remain separate measurements.
