What the current data do — and do not — establish

Established where supported: site identities, Epoch construction/chip observations, geographic grid jurisdiction, certain site-level utility/service evidence, two site-specific queue IDs, selected annual power snapshots, and selected cooling-equipment snapshots.

Not established by missingness: a missing queue ID does not prove no connection; an open telemetry task does not prove low load; a source marked available but not ingested does not prove a condition is absent.

Measurements remain separate: queue MW, utility service capacity, contracted demand, energized capacity, Epoch IT power, chip TDP, and measured site load are different quantities.

Independence matters: multiple records can descend from one document. The ledger should not interpret record count as independent corroboration.
