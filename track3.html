Track 3 Evidence Registry

A complete publication surface for the Track 3 evidence model: the 93-site frontier-AI reference universe, grid and connection research, service and regulatory evidence, construction and physical infrastructure, power observations, remote-sensing requirements, cooling, transformers, chip inventory and compute accounting, tenancy, source provenance, entity-resolution rules, geospatial policy, and governance references.

Evidence semantics
UNKNOWN, PENDING_RESEARCH, SOURCE_AVAILABLE_NOT_INGESTED and NOT_INGESTED are explicit states. They never become evidence of absence. Queue MW, utility service capacity, contracted demand, energized capacity, Epoch IT power, chip power and measured load remain separate measurements.

Track 3 definition & architecture

The canonical Track 3 document describes an auditable evidence graph for whether material AI compute can be accounted for through public physical, electrical, hardware and independent evidence.
