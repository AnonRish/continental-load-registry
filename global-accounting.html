RESIDUAL

Why the untraced pool remains unknown

The repository has the model, but a real certificate requires a closed population and verified observations. Public aggregate ownership/sales snapshots cannot be added together across overlapping dates to create a valid global inventory. Likewise, company-level ownership does not establish a physical location.

Current state: framework implemented; numerical residual not claimed.
